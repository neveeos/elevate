How to join

Registration takes about ten minutes. Keep your bank receipt ready.

  1. Choose a course

    Open the course to see the timetable, teacher and fee.

  2. Pay the fee

    Transfer the fee, or the first instalment where offered, to the school’s bank account.

  3. Register online

    Fill in your details and upload a photo or screenshot of your receipt.

  4. Get confirmed

    The school checks the payment and e-mails your confirmation and class link.

Questions

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Where do classes happen?

Live online, on Zoom, at the times shown for each intake (Pakistan time). You need a laptop or computer and a stable internet connection.

Is there a demo class?

No. Elevate courses are registered directly. Each course page shows what you will learn, the timetable and the teacher, and you can ask us anything on WhatsApp before you pay.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to the school’s account shown on the registration form. Upload your receipt when you register; your seat is confirmed after the payment is verified.

Can I pay in instalments?

Some courses allow instalments. If a course does, you can choose them on the registration form and you pay the first instalment to register.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a participation certificate from Kulsoom Hassan School when you complete the course.
